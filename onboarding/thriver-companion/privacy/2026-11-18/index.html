Privacy Policy

Here is what happens to the health information you choose to share in Thriver Companion.

What you share

The assessment asks for your name, email, age, how long you have felt unwell, symptoms, everyday activities, feelings and goals. Use the open questions to describe your own experience. Leave out other people’s names or medical details.

What your answers are for

We use your answers to build your Nervous System Health Assessment, choose relevant learning materials and help your coaching team support you. In the member platform, a client ID connects each dated assessment to your account. This lets you compare months without overwriting what you said before. We also keep a record of the policy versions you agreed to.

Who can see them

Your assigned coaching team and the service providers that help us store and deliver your assessment can handle the information needed for their work. If you book a call, the scheduling provider receives the booking details you enter. Completing onboarding does not give permission to publish your story, send marketing messages or train an AI model on your answers.

How long we keep information

We keep information as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Our website policy describes retention and deletion, including exceptions. It also explains how the website uses cookies and how information may be processed in the United States.

Your choices

You can leave before submitting. Without permission to use your health answers, we cannot produce a personalized assessment. You can ask us for a copy of your information, request a correction or deletion, or withdraw permission for future use. Email support and say which request you want to make. You do not need to include your symptoms in the email.

Who handles requests

CFS Recovery Inc. is responsible for this service. The CFS Recovery Privacy Policy gives more detail about your rights where you live, service providers and when disclosure may be required by law.
