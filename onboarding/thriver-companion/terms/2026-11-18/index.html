Terms and Conditions

These terms explain how to use Thriver Companion as part of your CFS Recovery program.

What the assessment can tell you

Your report brings together what you tell us about your symptoms and daily life. You and your Recovery Guide can use it to choose a starting point and see what changes from month to month. A score is a snapshot of your answers. Only a qualified healthcare professional can diagnose an illness or advise you about treatment.

Using your account

Use your own account and keep your sign-in details private. If you are filling this out for a child enrolled in a program, you must be the parent or guardian managing their account. Adults create and manage accounts. Contact support if someone else has access to yours.

Your program and materials

Completing the assessment does not buy a program or change your billing. Your enrollment terms govern fees, renewal and cancellation. You can use the lessons and worksheets for your own learning. Ask us before distributing paid materials or publishing another member’s story.

Health questions and urgent help

Bring new or worsening symptoms to a healthcare professional. If you need urgent medical help, contact local emergency services. Member stories describe individual experiences. Your experience and results may differ.

Which terms apply

CFS Recovery Inc. provides Thriver Companion. These Companion terms supplement the CFS Recovery Terms and Conditions linked below. The main terms cover payments, refunds, legal rights and how disputes are handled. Your program agreement also applies to your enrollment.
