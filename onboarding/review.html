    What gets submitted?
    1. Members answer 39 questions. They describe their recovery goal and where they want help in two separate answers.
    2. Age, illness duration in years and months, report details, and the two policy choices.
    3. Your answers stay in this tab. Reloading the page clears them.
    How the answers get used
    1. Capacity ratings, symptoms and activity patterns shape the member’s assessment snapshot.
    2. Members repeat the assessment monthly to see what changes and adjust their next steps.
    3. The first answer shapes an example of a difficult day. The goal chosen near the end selects five quotes from members who shared that goal.
    Which assessment is this?

    This demo uses the November 10 questionnaire and the updated report rules. The report shows physical, cognitive and emotional capacity and patterns.
